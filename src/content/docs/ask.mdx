Ask research questions about California law. Your question is first matched against
the full text of the codes on this site, and the most relevant retrieved sections are
passed to your chosen model as evidence — with citations back to the pages they came from.

## Bring your own key

Pick a provider, paste its API key, and ask away. The key is stored only in your
browser (localStorage) and requests go directly from your browser to the provider:

- **OpenRouter** (recommended — one key, hundreds of models, browser-friendly) —
  get a key at [openrouter.ai/keys](https://openrouter.ai/keys)
- **Sarvam AI** — get a key at [dashboard.sarvam.ai](https://dashboard.sarvam.ai)
  (model defaults to `sarvam-m`; other Sarvam models can be typed in)
- **Mistral AI** — get a key at [console.mistral.ai](https://console.mistral.ai)

:::caution[AI answers are not legal advice]
AI-generated research assistance may contain mistakes. Verify provisions, effective
dates, and applicability against the [official California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/)
before relying on them, and consult a qualified attorney for specific legal matters.
:::
